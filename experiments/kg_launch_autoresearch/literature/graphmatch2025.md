# GraphMatch (2025)

Primary source: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02849>

GraphMatch already constructs point-in-time subgraphs, versions node features,
filters candidates by activity time, and samples historical prediction times
for unseen-node recommendation. This kills a broad claim that historical graph
replay is new. A surviving contribution must concern external-KG transaction
time or a new estimator, not point-in-time interaction-graph construction.
