# MIND (ACL 2020)

Primary source: <https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.331/>

MIND contains timestamped news impressions and Wikidata-linked entities in
news titles and abstracts. The released entity and relation embeddings were
trained from a Wikidata subgraph. This makes MIND suitable for a bounded audit
of external-KG transaction-time drift, while the present experiment makes no
claim that MIND's supplied embeddings themselves contain future facts.
