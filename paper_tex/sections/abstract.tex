Recommender-systems research has a well-documented evaluation-trust problem. We study text-augmented sequential recommendation on Amazon Reviews 2023 (5-core, full-catalog, leave-last-out) under a pre-declared, artifact-gated discipline: version-controlled pre-declarations, a fail-closed build gate that recomputes all 177 artifact-gated table cells from released artifacts, comparator regeneration under disclosed environment caveats, and symmetric self-VOIDing adjudication --- one campaign remains VOID by its own frozen rule and is reported as such. At their exact strength, the findings are: (i) two pre-declared per-category point-estimate comparisons met a predeclared threshold relative to fixed published HSTU-BLaIR point estimates under the stated environment caveats (Musical\_Instruments fresh 5-seed CI lower bounds 0.04096/0.04083 vs 0.0406; Office\_Products 0.03033/0.03024 vs 0.0271 published and 0.0279 environment-matched regeneration) --- not SOTA and not paired superiority; (ii) a minimal gradient-active causal FIR residual is isolated from its identity control under matched initialization on three categories: Musical\_Instruments +0.002265 {[}0.001928, 0.002602{]}, Industrial\_and\_Scientific +0.002110 {[}0.001820, 0.002399{]}, and CDs\_and\_Vinyl +0.006150 {[}0.005849, 0.006450{]} (8/8 positive on each breadth category; Holm-corrected) --- internal filter-vs-identity contrasts, not external-comparator or SOTA claims; (iii) a Musical\_Instruments tail advantage of frozen text features survives a repaired estimand (+0.000420, 95\% CI {[}+0.000181, +0.000660{]}, Holm) but concentrates in the frequency-5 boundary group (excluding it, p = 0.52) --- mechanism unresolved, and the cross-dataset contrast did not replicate. Retractions, negative results, and design defects are reported alongside the positive findings; code, data derivatives, and the audit chain are released, with per-user sidecars where the environment permits.
