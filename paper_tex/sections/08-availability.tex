\section{Code and Data Availability}\label{sec:8}

All preprocessing, training, and evaluation code is available at \texttt{\_bestrec\_run/}. Pre-computed text-encoder caches for Video\_Games, Musical\_Instruments, Office\_Products, and Beauty\_and\_\allowbreak PC are covered by SHA256 in \texttt{RELEASE\_MANIFEST.json} (release \texttt{v0.9-audit-evidence}), with regeneration instructions in the protocol-parity appendix; MI confirmation result JSONs and per-user sidecars are tracked in the repository with per-file hashes; Office descriptive summary JSONs are manifest-backed, while Office and FIR-breadth per-user sidecars are local-only (untracked; hashed into each run's embedded manifest) and not part of any counted claim.
