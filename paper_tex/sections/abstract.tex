Recommender-systems research has a documented evaluation-trust problem: weak or mistuned baselines, non-comparable protocols, and unreproducible results repeatedly yield progress claims that do not survive scrutiny (Ferrari Dacrema et al., 2019). The lead contribution of this paper is methodological --- an executable, per-paper evaluation discipline under which every empirical finding below is produced and policed: \textbf{(i) version-controlled pre-declared protocols (git-committed before the runs; commit-ordered but carrying no independent external timestamp --- a stated limitation, \S\ref{sec:6.5})} (the comparator-facing confirmation was git-committed --- exact configuration, commands, data hashes, decision rule, claim wording --- before any confirmatory run, and executed on never-inspected fresh seeds); \textbf{(ii) a fail-closed artifact gate} (a manifest enumerates every cell of the artifact-gated result tables (175 cells across 15 claim families; two expository tables --- the dataset-statistics table in \S\ref{sec:4.1} and the supporting Appendix A.1 scan --- are converted from the canonical markdown outside this graph and are labeled as such in their provenance); a build step recomputes each cell from its source artifacts and fails if any printed value is not reproduced at its printed precision; values whose artifacts cannot be recomputed are retired rather than grandfathered, and published comparator numbers are carried as explicitly-labeled external constants); \textbf{(iii) a comparator-regeneration harness} (the strongest comparator's unmodified reference implementation executes locally via pure-PyTorch data-movement shims, so the published point estimate we gate against is regenerated by its own generating code rather than only transcribed --- environment-caveated single-run regenerations, \S\ref{sec:5.6}); and \textbf{(iv) symmetric, self-VOIDing adjudication} (pre-declared failures are published with the same prominence as passes: a second-category pre-declaration VOIDed itself when its own floor check detected a comparability failure, and the VOID stands even though the anomaly was later mechanistically explained, Appendix A.0; a redesigned successor pre-declaration with an environment-matched reference subsequently \textbf{passed} on fresh seeds, \S\ref{sec:5.2}). The empirical findings that follow are reported as demonstrations of this discipline.

We study text-augmented sequential recommendation on the Amazon Reviews 2023 (AR2023) benchmark \citep{hou2024blair} under a fixed protocol --- 5-core, full-catalog leave-last-out (LLOO), NDCG@10 --- built on an \textbf{HSTU-style pure-PyTorch implementation of the HSTU encoder, based on the published architecture} \citep{zhai2024hstu}. Our central architectural contribution --- the discipline's lead demonstration --- is a \textbf{strictly causal FIR temporal filter}: a zero-init gated depthwise convolution on the sequence embeddings that supplies an explicit local recency bias (as an FIR filter it has a learnable frequency response, so it can sharpen the short-horizon structure that BSARec's low-pass analysis of vanilla self-attention motivates us to restore). We are explicit about the novelty boundary. \emph{Not new}: filtering sequential representations, the frequency-domain motivation, and the attention-oversmoothing framing, all established by FMLP-Rec \citep{zhou2022fmlprec} and BSARec \citep{shin2024bsarec}. \emph{New}: the leak-free, left-causal FIR realization inserted before an HSTU-style stack under the all-position next-item objective, evaluated under full-catalog LLOO --- FMLP-Rec and BSARec use bidirectional sequence filters in their original formulations, and under our all-position next-item loss directly inserting such filters before each position's prediction would mix future positions, so we use a left-causal FIR realization. We pair it with \textbf{Text-Anchored Prototype Embeddings (TAPE)} --- a simple soft text-prototype additive capacity term inspired by semantic-ID and prototype methods (a continuous, decoder-free analogue of TIGER's discrete semantic IDs; \citealp{rajput2023tiger}): frozen k-means soft-assignments over item-text embeddings gating a learnable prototype table --- which we report honestly as a \emph{modest sub-additive} text contributor (+0.0009 single-flag), not a headline. We do not claim a wholly new recommender architecture. Our architectural additions are deliberately small: a causal FIR adaptation of prior frequency-filter ideas and a soft text-prototype additive term.

On AR2023 \textbf{Video\_Games} 5-core full-catalog LLOO, our full stack reaches \textbf{NDCG@10 = 0.0673 \ensuremath{\pm} 0.0003 (6-seed mean \ensuremath{\pm} std, seeds 20260608--13)}, a \textbf{+17.5\%} improvement over the published SASRec baseline (0.0573) on the identical protocol. We are explicit that \textbf{this win is architectural, not text-driven}: an ID-only ablation (no SBERT features, no text-sim bias, no prototypes) already reaches \textbf{\ensuremath{\approx}0.0656 (+14\% over published SASRec)}, and the full text stack adds only \textbf{+2.7\% overall} (5-seed +0.00178 \ensuremath{\pm} 0.00021). A controlled per-component ablation on the HSTU-style implementation isolates the levers: the time bias is the largest classical component (+0.0027), label smoothing (Szegedy et al., 2016) adds +0.0013, the causal filter adds +0.0015, while text-similarity bias is dead weight (\ensuremath{\pm}0.0001) and TAPE is sub-additive (+0.0009). The causal filter's gain is robust across kernel lengths K\ensuremath{\in}\{4,8,16,50\} (k16 marginally best, 5-seed 0.0676 \ensuremath{\pm} 0.0002). Crucially, \textbf{the causal filter --- not label smoothing --- is the larger contributor to the cross-category generalization (a package effect under the \S\ref{sec:3} initialization caveat, not a clean single-factor attribution)}: on a second category, \textbf{Musical\_Instruments}, a single-lever 5-seed isolation shows the filter contributes \textbf{+0.0025 (\ensuremath{\approx}80\% of the combined lift, \ensuremath{\approx}3\ensuremath{\times} label-smoothing's +0.0009)}, lifting the V2 stack to \textbf{0.0413 \ensuremath{\pm} 0.0005 (5-seed, exploratory)} over a 0.0383 HSTU base; and under a separate \textbf{pre-declared breadth campaign} (zero per-category tuning, fresh seeds, frozen decision rule), the filter's pre-declared same-seed 5-seed improvement (arms not initialization-paired, \S\ref{sec:5.3}) is positive with 95\% CIs excluding zero on two further categories (Industrial\_and\_Scientific +0.0024, CDs\_and\_Vinyl +0.0057) --- \textbf{four categories in total} (the frozen rule's paired \emph{interpretation} is withdrawn --- arms are not initialization-paired, \S\ref{sec:5.3}; the primary statement is the independent-arm Welch analysis, both 95\% CIs excluding zero: IS {[}+0.0019, +0.0029{]}, CDs {[}+0.0050, +0.0063{]}; the treatment is the FIR-plus-initialization/optimizer package, \S\ref{sec:3}). Under a \textbf{pre-declared, version-controlled confirmation} (git-committed protocol; five pre-specified never-inspected seeds per kernel; ten arm-runs), both kernels independently exceed the published HSTU-BLaIR point estimate for this category (0.0406; Liu 2025): fresh 5-seed 95\% CI lower bounds \textbf{0.04096 (K=16)} and \textbf{0.04083 (K=8)}, 10/10 seeds above. A \textbf{second category passed the redesigned pre-declaration}: on Office\_Products, gating against the environment-matched local regeneration of the comparator (best full-eval 0.0279, above the published 0.0271), both kernels' fresh 5-seed CI lower bounds clear both references (K=16 \textbf{0.03033}, K=8 \textbf{0.03024}; 10/10 seeds above; \S\ref{sec:5.2}). Because the comparator is a single-seed published number whose pinned official environment cannot execute on our hardware (its research path runs locally only as unpinned, shimmed, environment-caveated regenerations, \S\ref{sec:5.6}), this is a \textbf{per-category point-estimate comparison, not a paired superiority or general SOTA claim}.

Our second headline is a \textbf{dataset-conditional long-tail pattern} (an empirical pattern, not a law). Comparing the text stack to its ID-only ablation on the rare-item (train-frequency) tail stratum: text \textbf{wins the tail on the sparse-catalog Musical\_Instruments} (nominal train-frequency cohort --- \S\ref{sec:5.3} defect disclosures; 5-seed \ensuremath{\Delta} = \textbf{+0.000335 \ensuremath{\pm} 0.000195, 5/5 same-seed differences positive; independent-arm Welch 95\% CI {[}+0.000109, +0.000562{]} excludes 0}, replicated on HR), while \textbf{no significant tail difference was detected on dense Video\_Games or Beauty\_and\_PC} --- the former powered-equivalence claims are \textbf{retracted} (the arms are not initialization-paired and the margin was data-derived, \S\ref{sec:5.3}; Beauty is a 3-seed exploratory estimate). The MI tail win is \textbf{confirmed by a pre-declared, externally timestamped, repaired-estimand campaign} (TFV2, 2026-07-20: tie-safe cohort, zero-exposure targets separated --- they score exactly 0.0 in both arms --- 8-vs-8 independent arms; +0.000420, 95\% CI {[}+0.000181, +0.000660{]}, Holm-PASS); the \textbf{cross-dataset MI\ensuremath{-}VG contrast did not replicate as significant under that campaign} (+0.000247, p = 0.13; now descriptive only). An interaction-thinning \textbf{titration} yields a \textbf{double result} (level contrasts under a bundled intervention): the head/overall text advantage rises as interactions thin (strong NDCG rank trend, exact permutation p = 0.017, one adjacent reversal; HR trend not significant, p \ensuremath{\approx} 0.14) while the tail effect is trend-free (thinning Video\_Games to Musical\_Instruments' exact global density does not reproduce MI's tail win --- at 5 seeds that density-equivalent rung lands at a flat null, \ensuremath{\Delta} = \ensuremath{-}0.000108 \ensuremath{\pm} 0.000503, 1/5 seeds positive) --- so density is a tail \emph{correlate} only. A follow-up \textbf{user-mode titration} (thinning users, not interactions) moves in the predicted direction at MI-matched connectivity (dd = +0.000326 vs the full-density anchor; all five same-seed dd positive) but is \textbf{not significant under the corrected independent-arm analysis (p = 0.058, CI includes zero) --- a suggestive, descriptive result, downgraded 2026-07-19} (\S\ref{sec:5.4.2}); the 5\ensuremath{\times}5 matched-R1 level contrast (diff-of-diffs +0.000761, opposite signs) is likewise descriptive. These titration results are controlled intervention evidence on the dataset, not causal identification of the real-world process that generated it.

We do \textbf{not} match the published HSTU-BLaIR Video\_Games result (0.0760); we could not isolate the cause of that residual gap: the \emph{pinned} reference environment is not installable on our hardware (its CUDA/Triton kernels have no sm\_120 images --- proven via WSL), which blocks a faithful pinned reproduction. A late shimmed execution of the reference implementation's research path (\S\ref{sec:5.6}) regenerates the published Musical\_Instruments comparator locally (best full-eval NDCG@10 0.0406 --- exact; final epoch 0.0391) but was not run on Video\_Games, so there kernel numerics, training recipe, feature pipeline, and other system differences remain unseparated. We therefore frame the Video\_Games result as a competitive, fully-attributed, multi-seed contribution rather than a SOTA claim.
