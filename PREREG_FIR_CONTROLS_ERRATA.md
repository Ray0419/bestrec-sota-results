# Execution and custody disclosure — FIR active controls

This disclosure preserves the frozen `PREREG_FIR_CONTROLS` decision family and
the mechanical verdict `CTRL-ACTIVE-CONTROL-SUPPORTED`; it narrows the evidence
classification after the 2026-07-27 audit.

1. The campaign was designed after the learned-FIR versus identity outcome on the
   same Musical_Instruments split was known. It is an outcome-known internal
   mechanism study, not independent confirmation.
2. Training completed across a dirty evolving worktree and multiple commits. The
   result JSONs bind trainer/config/data hashes, but immutable attempt-level
   repository custody was not achieved.
3. The evaluator wrote `*.finaleval.users.npz`, while the original ignore rule
   covered `*.finaleval.perusers.npz`. Consequently, the 48 per-user sidecars and
   STARTED seals were ordinary untracked files during Stage 2. The suffix rule is
   corrected prospectively; exact files and hashes are preserved in the release
   inventory. This repair cannot retroactively create independent custody.
4. The mechanical adjudicator was the first reader used by this continuation and
   processed the family only after 48/48 final evaluations completed. Whether an
   author, IDE preview, external process, or other workspace exposed any endpoint
   earlier cannot be established mechanically and remains an author-verification
   item.
5. Fixed MA and fixed HP reduce to algebraically redundant scalar/sign function
   classes in this implementation. Their two contrasts remain reported because the
   frozen family cannot be amended after TEST, but they are not counted as
   independent mechanism controls. The channel-shared and parameter-matched
   nonlinear arms are the distinct active controls.
6. Stage-2 provenance hashed raw worktree source bytes. The trainer was captured
   with Windows CRLF endings (`7bdde0...51f4`), whereas its exact Git/LF content
   hashes to `5620d4...d854`. The adjudicator now verifies both facts: every
   immutable final-evaluation record must retain the frozen raw hash, and the
   current source must match the frozen canonical LF hash. This portability repair
   changes no result file or endpoint and prevents a clean checkout from failing
   solely because of line-ending representation.

The result supports causal temporal mixing/activity because every active arm
improves identity. Learned taps beat both redundant fixed arms but do not separate
from the shared or nonlinear controls. A retained test is not evidence of
equivalence, and learned per-channel FIR coefficients are not uniquely necessary
under this configuration.
