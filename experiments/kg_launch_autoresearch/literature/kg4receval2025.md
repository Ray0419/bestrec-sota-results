# KG4RecEval (TOIS 2025)

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.03164

KG4RecEval removes, decreases, and randomly distorts KG knowledge across several models and datasets. Accuracy often does not fall, including for users with very few interactions. This kills a generic "KG helps cold start" premise. It does not test future-born zero-interaction items, linkability selection, or cutoff-specific fact provenance.
