# ColdGPT (RecSys 2023)

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.14462

ColdGPT pretrains an item-attribute graph using contents, warm purchase sequences, and warm review text, then inserts unseen items through their attributes. It is a strong strict-item-cold baseline and is efficient at inference. Its evaluation filters items with insufficient content; Home-SCS test items introduce almost no new attribute vocabulary. It therefore does not answer launch-time provenance or full-catalog linkability.
