# Firzen (ICDE 2024)

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07654

Firzen combines a collaborative KG, item-item multimodal graphs, and a user co-occurrence graph, targeting warm and strict-cold items together. It randomly assigns 20 percent of items to cold validation/test sets. This is the main comparison for multimodal plus KG strict cold start, but the split is not prospective and relation observation time is not audited.
