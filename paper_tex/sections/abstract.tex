Recommender-systems research has a well-documented evaluation-trust problem: weak or mistuned baselines, non-comparable protocols, and post-hoc claims. We study text-augmented sequential recommendation on Amazon Reviews 2023 (5-core, full-catalog, leave-last-out) and organize the entire study around a pre-declared, artifact-gated evaluation apparatus: version-controlled pre-declarations (git-committed; the latest also carries OpenTimestamps proofs whose earliest Bitcoin attestation postdates its first result --- chronology in \S\ref{sec:5.3}), a fail-closed build gate recomputing all 175 artifact-gated table cells from released artifacts, comparator regeneration under disclosed environment caveats, and symmetric self-VOIDing adjudication --- one campaign remains VOID by its own frozen rule and is reported as such. Substantive findings, stated at their exact strength: (i) two pre-declared per-category point-estimate comparisons pass against published HSTU-BLaIR numbers (Musical\_Instruments fresh 5-seed CI lower bounds 0.04096/0.04083 vs 0.0406; Office\_Products 0.03033/0.03024 vs 0.0271 published and 0.0279 environment-matched regeneration) --- point-estimate comparisons, not SOTA and not paired superiority; (ii) a leak-free causal FIR filter --- as the FIR-plus-initialization/optimizer package, the bundle our design can attribute (\S\ref{sec:3}) --- is supported on four categories and replicated by an independent-arm rerun under Git-committed frozen rules (8-vs-8; Industrial\_and\_Scientific +0.002131, CDs\_and\_Vinyl +0.005770; Holm-corrected; outcome-visible, so not labeled confirmatory --- \S\ref{sec:5.3}); (iii) the Musical\_Instruments tail advantage of frozen text features survives a repaired estimand --- tie-safe cohorts with zero-exposure targets separated (+0.000420, 95\% CI {[}+0.000181, +0.000660{]}, Holm) --- but concentrates in the frequency-5 boundary group (excluding it: +0.000071, p = 0.52): a \textbf{frequency-5-heavy pattern, not a smooth rare-item benefit}; the cross-dataset contrast did not replicate and earlier equivalence claims are retracted. Every retraction, negative result, and design defect is reported in full; code, data derivatives, per-user sidecars, and the adversarial audit chain are public.
