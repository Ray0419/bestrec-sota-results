\subsection{The Musical\_Instruments frequency-5 tail case (cross-dataset heterogeneity not established)}\label{sec:5.3}


Beyond \emph{whether} text helps overall (modestly: +2.7\% on Video\_Games, \S\ref{sec:5.1}), we ask \emph{where this additive frozen-text construction helps} (only early additive fusion of frozen text features is tested --- \S\ref{sec:2.3} scope note). \texttt{evaluate()} reports \texttt{by\_popularity} NDCG@10 over nominal train-frequency terciles \{tail, mid, head\} (bucketed by TRAIN frequency only, so no validation/test frequencies enter the bucketing; two cohort-definition defects are disclosed below), and we compare the full text stack against an ID-only ablation on the \textbf{same split and seed numbers}. \textbf{Randomization disclosure (load-bearing; added 2026-07-19).} Same-numbered seeds do \textbf{not} make the two arms a matched pair. The driver seeds Python/NumPy/Torch once at startup; the text arm then constructs its SBERT projection and distillation head \textbf{before} the shared backbone, so the backbone initialization, the DataLoader shuffle stream, and the dropout stream all differ between same-seed text and ID runs (direct constructor probe at one seed: \texttt{item\_emb} identical; \texttt{pos\_emb} max |\ensuremath{\Delta}| \ensuremath{\approx} 0.104; first \texttt{uvqk} \ensuremath{\approx} 0.079; \texttt{out} \ensuremath{\approx} 0.079). Every ``paired, per-seed'' label previously attached to Table 1d is therefore \textbf{withdrawn}: the tabulated per-seed differences are same-seed-number differences of independently initialized arms (descriptive), and all inference below is \textbf{independent-arm (Welch)}. The prior paired-CI/MDE/TOST analyses are retracted (point 2 below), and their artifact-graph nodes are tombstoned.

\subsection{Titration: thinning tracks the head effect but not the tail effect (a double/refuting result; level contrasts under a bundled intervention)}\label{sec:5.4}


To test whether \emph{global interaction density} (the most obvious covariate separating sparse MI from dense VG) drives the tail pattern, we ran a pre-declared \textbf{interaction-thinning titration}: a six-rung ladder thinning Video\_Games' interaction graph toward progressively lower density (down to MI's exact global density at \ensuremath{\rho}=0.66), re-measuring the \texttt{text - ID} head and tail \ensuremath{\Delta} at each rung (best-by-val, n\_eval fixed at 94,762 every rung). The result \textbf{splits}, and we report it as a \emph{double} result --- confirming on one stratum, refuting on the other:


\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{HEAD = strong falling-density rank trend on NDCG (5-seed-locked across every rung; not strictly monotone).} Head \ensuremath{\Delta} rises overall as VG thins --- per-rung 5-seed means down the density ladder \ensuremath{\rho} = 1.0 \ensuremath{\rightarrow} 0.94 \ensuremath{\rightarrow} 0.91 \ensuremath{\rightarrow} 0.88 \ensuremath{\rightarrow} 0.78 \ensuremath{\rightarrow} 0.66: \textbf{+0.00221 \ensuremath{\rightarrow} +0.00239 \ensuremath{\rightarrow} +0.00254 \ensuremath{\rightarrow} +0.00252 \ensuremath{\rightarrow} +0.00266 \ensuremath{\rightarrow} +0.00354}, all six rungs 5/5 seeds positive; \textbf{Spearman \ensuremath{\rho_s}(head \ensuremath{\Delta} vs density) = \ensuremath{-}0.94 on NDCG (exact two-sided permutation p = 0.017)} with one adjacent reversal (+0.002544 \ensuremath{\rightarrow} +0.002520), and the \textbf{HR trend is directionally consistent but not significant (\ensuremath{\rho_s} = \ensuremath{-}0.71, exact p \ensuremath{\approx} 0.14)}. \ensuremath{\Rightarrow} The head/overall text-complementarity rises under this bundled thinning intervention (a rank trend over six rungs on one fixed draw, no multiplicity correction; not strict monotonicity and not component-level causal attribution). (Every rung is 5 seeds \{20260608--12\}.)
\item
  \textbf{TAIL = REFUTED dose-response (5-seed-locked).} Tail \ensuremath{\Delta} is non-monotone and trend-free across the same rungs --- 5-seed means \textbf{\ensuremath{-}0.000148 \ensuremath{\rightarrow} +0.000165 \ensuremath{\rightarrow} +0.000039 \ensuremath{\rightarrow} +0.000537 (\ensuremath{\rho}=0.88) \ensuremath{\rightarrow} +0.000056 \ensuremath{\rightarrow} \ensuremath{-}0.000108 \ensuremath{\pm} 0.000503 (\ensuremath{\rho}=0.66, the MI-equivalent density rung)}; per-rung positive-seed counts 2/5, 4/5, 3/5, 4/5, 2/5, 1/5 --- no rung clears MI's +0.000335/5-of-5 bar, and \textbf{Spearman \ensuremath{\rho_s}(tail \ensuremath{\Delta} vs density) = \ensuremath{-}0.14 (n.s., 5 seeds)}. Thinning VG to MI's exact global density does \emph{not} reproduce MI's +0.000335 tail \emph{win} --- the MI-equivalent \ensuremath{\rho}=0.66 rung sits at a flat null straddling 0 (1/5 seeds positive), no further toward MI's positive value than full-density VG. \textbf{(Honesty note:} this rung was \ensuremath{-}0.000450 at 2 seeds and ``the most-negative thinned point''; filling it to 5 seeds regressed it to \ensuremath{-}0.000108 \ensuremath{\pm} 0.000503, dissolving the ``trough'' sub-claim. (The reproducible \ensuremath{\rho}=0.88 bump is non-monotone and does not survive a Bonferroni correction across the six rungs; we record it but do not headline it as a crossing.)
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Screening log of capacity-adding probes (a search record, not powered exclusions)}\label{sec:5.5}


A central empirical finding of this work is \textbf{asymmetric}: the only levers that moved test NDCG@10 were \emph{capacity-restricting} (label smoothing, the causal FIR filter) or classical inductive biases already in Table 1 (the time bias); \textbf{no capacity-\emph{adding} probe we tried showed a benefit at its tested power (mostly single-seed).} We document the full set here as a first-class result --- negatives are part of the contribution, and several are \emph{interpretable} negatives in which a learnable scalar, free to engage the mechanism, was driven by the optimizer to (or past) zero. This is observational evidence --- most rows are single-seed probes (power note below) --- that the in-environment ceiling was not left unexplored on these axes; the residual gap to the published HSTU-BLaIR 0.0760 could not be isolated (the pinned reference environment is not installable here, and the shimmed local execution of \S\ref{sec:5.6} did not include the Video\_Games configuration); what the probe set shows is that none of the tested probes (mostly single-seed; Table 2 power note) produced a gain --- observations that leave the gap unexplained, not exclusions.


All entries are best-by-val test NDCG@10, full-catalog eval (n\_eval = 94,762 on Video\_Games), on the same fixed protocol. \ensuremath{\Delta} is versus the stated base; for the three learnable-scalar probes (W1/X1/Y1) we report the absolute single-seed test next to its base band, since the headline test is statistically indistinguishable from the base and the \emph{learned scalar} is the load-bearing readout. \subsection{Executing the reference implementation locally (post-hoc comparator regeneration)}\label{sec:5.6}

Late in this work we succeeded in running the \textbf{reference implementation itself} --- the HSTU-BLaIR repository at its released commit, unmodified (clean git tree): its preprocessing, its gin configurations, its trainer, and its eval protocol --- end-to-end on our hardware. The pinned environment (torch 2.2.2 / fbgemm\_gpu 0.6.0) remains uninstallable here (no sm\_120 kernel images, no Windows wheels); what makes execution possible under our unpinned torch 2.11 environment is that the repository's research path uses exactly \textbf{three} \texttt{fbgemm} operators, all pure data movement (\texttt{asynchronous\_complete\_cumsum}, \texttt{jagged\_to\_padded\_dense}, \texttt{dense\_to\_jagged}), which we re-implement in plain PyTorch with self-tested forward/gradient parity, plus a world-size-1 identity DDP wrapper and logging/data-acquisition patches (full shim inventory, per-run provenance, and artifacts: \texttt{THEIRS\_ON\_OURS\_REPORT.md}).
