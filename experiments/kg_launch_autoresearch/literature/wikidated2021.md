# Wikidated 1.0 (2021)

Primary source: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.05003>

Wikidated demonstrates that Wikidata revision history can reconstruct how the
knowledge graph changed over time. It occupies revision-aware KG extraction;
the possible contribution here is its model-agnostic integration into strict
cold-start recommendation evaluation and a downstream bitemporal correction.
